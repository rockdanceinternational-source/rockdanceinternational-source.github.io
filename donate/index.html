Support Up Rock culture

Donations to Rock Dance International pay for five things, and all of them are Up Rock.

Tell me when donations openFollow on Instagram

Where your donation goes

  • The website Hosting rockdanceinternational.com and translating it, with every source linked.
  • Research Anthropological research into Up Rock and the communities that dance it.
  • Interviews Interviews with the OGs, the old school and the new generation of rockers, recorded and kept so their own words are not lost.
  • Artists and crews Support for the artists and crews who teach Up Rock and battle in their own cities.
  • The event Growing Rock Dance International, an event for Up Rock culture only.

Why one event for Up Rock only

The idea is to bring together practitioners, experts and pioneers. To learn the fundamentals from each other and keep the music and the dance in the room. The 2027 edition is on Saturday, November 20, 2027, in Barcelona, Spain: see the event. More info coming soon.

When you can donate

Donations open once the Asociación Rock Dance International, a non-profit association, completes its registration in Spain. We will announce it on Instagram, at @rockdanceinternational, and if you prefer email, use the button at the top of this page.

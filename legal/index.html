This site belongs to the Asociación Rock Dance International (in formation), a non-profit association. Contact: rockdanceinternational@gmail.com.

The site uses no cookies and no tracking. GitHub Pages hosts the website. Cloudflare receives newsletter forms and stores the list. These providers process technical connection data to deliver their services.

Newsletter: if you subscribe, the association is responsible for your data. We keep your name or aka, email, country, your crew if you give it, and the date you subscribed, only to send you news about Rock Dance International and its events. The legal basis is your consent; you can withdraw it at any time by writing to rockdanceinternational@gmail.com, and we delete you from the list. The list is stored with Cloudflare, in its Western Europe region, and we give it to no one.

If you write to us, we use your address only to answer you and never pass it on. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at the same address, and you may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

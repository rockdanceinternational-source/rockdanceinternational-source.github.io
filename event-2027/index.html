RDI - Rock Dance International 2027

Saturday, November 20, 2027 · Barcelona, Spain. One day for Up Rock only, open to rockers from every country. More info coming soon.

Get the news first

The day

  • Rock battles Face to face and without touching, the way the fundamentals describe.
  • Apache Lines Two lines of rockers face each other, one against one or crew against crew. How an Apache Line runs.
  • Call-outs A call-out is a challenge: one rocker names another and the two face off.
  • Up Rock Showcase Contest Best showcase or routine: a prepared choreography, judged apart from the battles.
  • Workshops Freestyle, Jerks & Burns, for every level, including dancers who come from other styles.
  • Open talk Where the dance comes from and where it is going.

New to the dance? Start with what Up Rock is and its fundamentals.

Four titles

  • Best Freestyle Free dancing on the beat, between the attacks.
  • Best Jerk The sudden drop and rock of the body, on the break.
  • Best Burn The rocker who turns the record into the most creative gestures against the other.

Prince & Princess Up Rock

The fourth title goes to the two rockers, one man and one woman, who best combine the three fundamentals in full: Freestyle, Jerks & Burns.

The prize is the red RDI vest, with the RDI patch on the back and the Prince Up Rock or Princess Up Rock badge over the heart.

Judges, the full rules and the prizes for the other three titles: coming soon.

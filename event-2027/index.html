Rock Dance International 2027

Saturday, November 20, 2027 · Spain. One day for Up Rock only, open to rockers from every country. More info coming soon.

Get the news first

Face to face. On the record. Nobody gets touched.

Four titles

  • Best Freestyle Free dancing on the beat, between the attacks.
  • Best Jerk The sudden drop and rock of the body, on the break.
  • Best Burn The rocker who turns the record into the most creative gestures against the other.

Prince / Princess of Up Rock

The fourth title of the day. Rules, judges and prizes for all four: coming soon.

The day

  • Rock battles. Face to face and without touching, the way the fundamentals describe.
  • Apache Lines. Two lines of rockers face each other, one against one or crew against crew. How an Apache Line runs.
  • Call-outs. A call-out is a challenge: one rocker names another and the two face off.
  • Workshops. Up Rock classes for every level, including dancers who come from other styles.
  • An open talk about where the dance comes from and where it is going.

Up Rock crews

A crew is a named group of rockers who battle as a team and wear the name on their backs. In the 1970s dozens of Up Rock crews battled in Brooklyn and the Bronx; today crews from Russia and Poland to Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, China and France carry the dance.

Each crew gets its page when its story is checked from start to finish: name, year, members, colors and the sources behind them.

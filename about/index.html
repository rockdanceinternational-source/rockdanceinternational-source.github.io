About RDI - Rock Dance International

RDI - Rock Dance International is a non-profit association devoted to one dance: Up Rock, also called Rock Dance.

What we do

  • Document the history of the dance and keep the testimony of the people who made it.
  • Teach it at every level and to every age, including dancers who come from other styles.
  • Connect rockers, crews, DJs and organizers across countries.
  • Help teachers, judges and organizers make a decent living from it.
  • Use the dance to bring neighborhoods and generations together.

The motto for passing the dance on:

His motto for passing the dance on: share what you know instead of keeping it to yourself.

How we check what we publish

The sources behind this site are listed on the sources page, each linked to the original. Most are interviews with rockers; the rest are archived crew websites, books and press. Each page links to at most one specific source entry. Quotes keep the speaker’s words and name; our reading of the lesson is separate.

More detail
  • Our study archive. We keep a private copy of the interviews for study, so nothing gets lost. We never publish those copies: the site sends you to the originals.
  • Quotes we print. A spoken quote goes on the site only when it reads the same in two independent transcriptions. When it does not, we tell what the person said in our own words and link the minute.
  • When accounts differ. Where Up Rock started, who named it, when the jerk came in: the people who were there remember it differently. The pages say so when they differ, and the sources page lets anyone check each account.

Work with us

Crews, DJs, organizers, researchers and institutions can write to rockdanceinternational@gmail.com. If you have photos, flyers, videos or memories of Up Rock that should be kept, we would like to hear from you. Institutions and researchers will find the whole archive behind this site on the sources page.

2015 · Rock'n'Raw

According to a 2019 Polish article, Kostek, Andy, Bożna, Ewa and Patrik founded the crew Rock’n’Raw (@rocknrawrockers) in 2015, and its 4th anniversary was set for December 7, 2019, in Warsaw. At its 10th Anniversary Jam, the Dynasty Rockers battled BVD. According to Kostek, the two crews are connected: RWF turned into Rock’n’Raw.

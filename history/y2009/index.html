2009 · Mastermind returns

In 2009 Mighty Mike and Ringo, with the blessing of original members, started a new generation of the Mastermind Rockers, the Williamsburg crew. The crew says it has since taken the dance to Japan, Taiwan, South Korea and Poland. Frankie Viejo and Fano had founded the crew in 1977, according to its website, mastermindrockers.com; on Instagram it is @mastermindrockers.

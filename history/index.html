Up Rock history: where the dance began

Up Rock was born on the streets of Bushwick, Brooklyn, in the late 1960s. The Dynasty Rockers' own accounts place its birth there in 1968. This timeline follows the main line of the story, from the first street corners to the rockers of Russia, Poland and Japan.

The 1990s: looking for the originals

In the 1990s Ralph “King Uprock” Casanova brought the dance back, at the head of a small group that, by Break Easy’s account, included Mr. Loose, Burn One and Charlie Uprock. Most of the original dancers had stopped about a decade before; he looked for them, brought them in as judges and began to teach again. Some rockers call it the rebirth.

2010 to 2019: Russia, Poland and Asia

From about 2010 Russian dancers, FT among them, invited the Dynasty Rockers to Russia, and by the crew’s account the visits went on almost every year until the pandemic. Rockers from Dynasty and Mastermind visited Poland, and crews there and in Asia grew their own scenes.

Today

For more than two decades Ralph “King Uprock” Casanova has been the one carrying the dance, with the Dynasty Rockers and the Brooklyn Arts Council, in New York and abroad. Today the dance has crews on several continents. Ralph says Dynasty has members in Russia, Las Vegas, Boston, China, Korea, Spain (Moysex, a Dynasty Rocker since 2006) and Colombia.

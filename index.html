Up Rock in one minute

A battle dance from Bushwick, Brooklyn, in the late 1960s. Two rockers face off to Funk and Soul records and improvise on the music: burn or get burned. Nobody gets touched.

  • Freestyle Free dancing on the beat between attacks.
  • Jerk Sudden drops and rocks of the body. The basic move.
  • Burn The rocker’s own gesture, made out of the music, to burn the other one.

Ralph remembers the first time he saw it: a street gang dancing on the corner of Cornelia and Wilson, in Bushwick.
